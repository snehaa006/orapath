What is Orapath?

Orapath is a female led initiative that aims to inspire and guide ambitious female youth through panels and workshops providing support for their endeavours.

 What do we aim to provide? 

We aim to provide self development courses such as business mentorship from industry experts through panels and workshops and Q&A sessions with successful individuals. 

Join Now

Why Was Orapath created?

As female saudi youth, we realized that there are many barriers between youth gaining  experience or an idea of an industry of interest when deciding possible career paths.  We have overcome these challenges by providing a network, and path for the youth to pursue their interests and gain an understanding of what the specific interest, from the perspective of a professional, might look like from the panel and workshop. 

Mission Statement

Orapath guides ambitious young women by providing industry specific mentorship, interactive panels, and workshops. Through enriching conversation, guidance, and support, we will equip the next generation of female leaders with the knowledge, and ambition they need to pursue their prospective goals.

Educate - Guide - Empower

Vision Statement

To cultivate an environment where young women are inspired, supported, and leading in their field of choice with a reliable network and community that champions their success.
