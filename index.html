ORAPATH

Orapath is a female led initiative that aims  to inspire and guide ambitious female youth through panels and workshops supporting their endeavors.

Our Mission & Vision

Orapath guides ambitious young women by providing industry specific mentorship, interactive panels, and workshops. Through enriching conversation, guidance, and support, we will equip the next generation of female leaders with the knowledge, and ambition they need to pursue their prospective goals.

Orapath provides panel sessions and workshops led by female industry professional within a certain field. The panel can range from 1-2 hours per session and is a safe platform for individuals to truly navigate through what specific industry might suit them.
